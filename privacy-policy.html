Privacy Policy

Belleza Beauty School App

Effective date: 13 October, 2026
Last updated: 13 October, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Belleza Beauty School ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the Belleza Beauty School mobile app (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Who uses the App

The App is for two groups of users:

  • Staff and administrators of Belleza Beauty School, who manage students, admissions, fees, courses, tasks and reports.
  • Students, who access courses, tests, resources, certificates, announcements and support.

Accounts are created or approved by the school. The App is not intended for children under 13.

2. Information we collect

Account information: name, email address, phone number, login credentials (passwords are never stored on your device), role and branch.

Student information: admission details, enrolled courses and batches, attendance and test attempts and results, fee and payment records, certificates issued.

Staff and business information: leads and enquiries, tasks, projects, reminders, reports, access permissions.

Content you provide: messages sent in team and project chat, support tickets, and files or images you choose to upload (for example resources or profile pictures).

Device and technical information: device type and operating system, app version, and basic diagnostic data needed to keep the App working and secure.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or microphone/camera data in the background. The App accesses your files or photos only when you choose to attach or upload them.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To run school operations: admissions, courses, tests, fees, certificates and reports.
  • To enable chat, tasks, reminders and support tickets.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot problems.
  • To communicate important updates and announcements.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Information stored on your device

Your sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). Some preferences and cached data may be kept in local app storage. Uninstalling the App or signing out removes this local data.

5. How information is shared

We share information only when needed:

  • Within the school: staff can see information according to the permissions assigned to their role.
  • Service providers: hosting, infrastructure and support providers who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety and security.
  • Documents you share: certificates and reports you export or share through your device's share sheet go wherever you send them.

6. Data transfer and security

Data is transmitted to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and live chat uses secure real-time connections. We use access controls and secure token-based authentication. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed for school records, legal, accounting and dispute-resolution purposes. After that it is deleted or anonymised.

8. Your rights and choices

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by contacting us at the address below. Some records (for example fee and certificate records) may need to be retained by law. You can also request account deletion through the same contact.

Account deletion: email schoolbelleza@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject "Delete my account". We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version. Significant changes will be communicated in the App or by email.
